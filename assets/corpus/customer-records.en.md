# Customer Records (Sample Data)

> Purpose: sample customer records used to demonstrate and test the PII redaction
> feature (FR-3). All names, addresses, phone numbers, national IDs and payment
> card numbers in this document are fictitious and used for testing only.

## 1. Record Format

1.1 Each customer record contains an identifier, a name, an email address, a phone number, a national ID and, for some records, a payment card number.

1.2 The record identifier follows the format C-1001, C-1002 and so on.

1.3 Email addresses use the format firstname.lastname@example.com.

1.4 Phone numbers appear in local and international formats, including Chinese mobile numbers.

1.5 National ID numbers are eighteen-digit identity numbers.

1.6 Payment card numbers in this file are valid test numbers that satisfy the Luhn checksum.

1.7 Records are stored in the customer relationship management system.

1.8 Access to customer personal data is restricted to authorised staff.

1.9 Customer personal data must never be displayed to third parties without consent.

1.10 When customer data appears in support tickets or reports, it must be redacted.

1.11 The email address is used for order notifications and account recovery.

1.12 The phone number is used for delivery and support contact.

1.13 The national ID is used for identity verification and tax invoicing.

1.14 Payment card numbers are used for billing and are never stored in plain text.

1.15 Customer records are retained according to the data retention schedule.

1.16 Any document containing these records must be handled as sensitive.

## 3. Redaction Requirements

3.1 Email addresses in any output must be redacted.

3.2 Phone numbers in any output must be redacted.

3.3 National ID numbers in any output must be redacted.

3.4 Payment card numbers in any output must be redacted.

3.5 Redacted values are replaced with a placeholder indicating the data type.

3.6 Redaction applies to chat answers, support tickets and log output.

3.7 The number of redaction hits is counted for monitoring.

3.8 Redaction rules are configured centrally and can be adjusted.

3.9 Sample queries may ask for customer contact details.

3.10 All answers grounded in these records must respect the redaction rules.

3.11 Customer personal data must not be echoed back to the caller unredacted.

3.12 The redaction requirement applies regardless of the language of the query.

3.13 Records must be retrieved only by authorised staff with a business need.

3.14 This sample data is for testing only and is not real customer information.

3.15 Questions about the redaction behaviour should be raised with the privacy team.
